電源

SPEAK UP IN ENGLISH英文自我介紹與交流用語

現場以英文進行。這一區本身就是英文素材，所以直接顯示。方括號內換成自己的資訊。

30 秒自我介紹
Hi, I'm Allen from Weblink in Taiwan. We're an IT distributor working with [number] resellers across Taiwan, and I focus on [Windows and commercial PCs].
This year our big push is helping SMB customers move off Windows 10 onto Windows 11 and AI PCs.
I'm here to learn how partners in other markets are doing it, and I'd love to hear what's working for you.
在場次中提問 · ASKING A QUESTION
  • Thanks for a great session. I'm Allen from Weblink, a distributor in Taiwan. My question is…
  • Could you share where partners can download that material?
  • Would it be possible to get a copy of the slides after the session?
  • Could you give an example of how a partner in Asia Pacific applied this?
交流破冰 · NETWORKING
  • What has been your most effective way to reach SMB customers this year?
  • How are your customers reacting to Copilot+ PCs?
  • Which co-marketing activity gave you the best return?
  • Is this your first time at Power On?
分組討論 · GROUP WORK
  • Shall I take notes for our group?
  • In Taiwan, we usually work through resellers, so the distributor's role is…
  • Can we agree on one target segment before we move on?
  • We have about five minutes left. Let's lock in the numbers.
卡丁車與晚宴 · KART NIGHT
  • Good race! Which group were you in?
  • Could we connect on LinkedIn? I'd love to compare notes after the event.
  • Would you be open to a quick call next month to share what's working?
  • Congratulations on the podium!
